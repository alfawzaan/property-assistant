# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## About the Agency
We are a premier real estate brokerage connecting verified buyers and tenants with approved residential and commercial properties in prime locations (Lekki, Ikoyi, Victoria Island, Ikeja, and Abuja).

## Buying a Property
- **How does buying work through our agency?**
  1. Browse and select verified properties for sale.
  2. Schedule an on-site inspection with our area sales agent.
  3. Submit an offer / letter of intent (LOI) through your agent.
  4. Physical inspection of original title documents at our corporate office with our legal team.
  5. Contract of Sale signing, payment settlement, and handover of title deeds.
- **Can I reserve a property?**
  Yes, sellers may accept a formal holding deposit (handled via secure escrow or agency terms) while title searches and sales agreements are finalized.

## Inspections & Viewing Fees
- **Why is there an inspection fee?**
  A standard inspection fee (typically ₦5,000 for residential properties) covers dedicated agent logistics and guarantees private, one-on-one viewing time.
- **Is the fee refundable?**
  Inspection fees are strictly non-refundable once a viewing appointment is confirmed and an agent is dispatched.
- **How do I pay?**
  Payments are processed securely via Paystack using debit card, bank transfer, or USSD. We never accept cash in hand or transfers to personal agent bank accounts.

## Tenancy & Purchase Terms
- **Standard Rental Terms**: Annual rent upfront. Standard statutory fees include 10% Agency fee, 10% Legal agreement fee, and a refundable Caution deposit.
- **Title Documents & Verification**: We do not send title documents, Surveys, or Certificate of Occupancy (C of O) copies over chat. All document inspections take place at our corporate office with our legal team prior to closing.

## Working with Agents
- Each client is assigned a dedicated agent for their chosen neighborhood to coordinate viewings, submit offers, and answer specific property questions.
