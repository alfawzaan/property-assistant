# Agency Operational Policies

## 1. Inspection Fee & Payment Policy
- **Fee Amount**: Approved residential viewings carry a flat ₦5,000 fee per inspection day (up to 3 properties in the same neighborhood). Commercial properties carry a ₦10,000 fee.
- **Paystack Exclusivity**: All fees must be initiated through the official Paystack hosted checkout link generated by the system.
- **Verification Rule**: Never confirm an inspection or issue a receipt until Paystack verification returns `verified_success`. Bank SMS alerts, screenshot receipts, or customer claims are not accepted.
- **Refunds**: Non-refundable once scheduled. If an agent cancels or a property becomes unavailable before the visit, the fee may be credited toward a rescheduled date.

## 2. Agent Assignment & Ownership
- **Existing Relationships**: If a returning lead already has an assigned agent, retain that agent.
- **New Leads**: Unassigned leads are routed based on neighborhood specialization.
- **Reassignment Requests**: If a client requests a different agent, log the request and hand off to the operations manager. Do not promise or approve reassignments directly in chat.

## 3. Human Escalation Rules
Escalate immediately to a human agent via `create_handoff` when:
1. The prospect asks for title verification, land registry searches, Governor's Consent, or C of O documents.
2. The prospect wishes to make an official offer or sign a tenancy agreement.
3. The prospect expresses dissatisfaction, reports an agent misconduct issue, or requests a refund.
4. The system tools fail or an inventory listing has conflicting price/availability data.
